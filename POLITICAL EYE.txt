# POLITICAL EYE

## Comprehensive Data Source Registry

### Instructions to Manus

Political Eye must be built around a multi-source research architecture.

Do NOT treat this as a list of websites to scrape.

Treat this as a **source-of-truth hierarchy and research acquisition framework**.

For every important fact, Political Eye should attempt to identify:

1. Primary source
2. Secondary independent source
3. Supporting source
4. Historical source
5. Latest available update

Where sources disagree, retain the disagreement and flag it.

Never silently choose one source and discard the others.

---

# 1. SOURCE HIERARCHY

Every source must be assigned a reliability tier.

## TIER 1 — PRIMARY / OFFICIAL

These should normally be the canonical sources for factual records:

* Election Commission of India
* State Election Commissions
* Lok Sabha / Parliament
* Rajya Sabha
* State Legislative Assemblies
* Government ministries
* State government departments
* Comptroller and Auditor General
* Supreme Court of India

* PIB

* official budget documents
* official scheme dashboards
* official statistical agencies
* RBI
* SEBI
* MCA
* NITI Aayog
* MoSPI
* NCRB
* official procurement portals
* official political-party disclosures

## TIER 2 — INSTITUTIONAL / RESEARCH

Use for structured cross-checking:

* ADR
* MyNeta
* PRS India
* Lokniti-CSDS
* India Data Portal
* Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy where legally accessible
* academic institutions
* recognised policy research organisations

## TIER 3 — ESTABLISHED JOURNALISM

Use primarily for:

* breaking events
* political developments
* investigations
* interviews
* controversies
* political statements
* protests
* resignations
* defections
* events not yet documented through official records

Use multiple independent publications where possible.

## TIER 4 — OFFICIAL SOCIAL MEDIA

Use verified accounts of:

* politicians
* political parties
* Prime Minister's Office
* President's Office
* ministries
* state governments
* Chief Ministers
* Election Commission
* Parliament
* courts

Use primarily to establish:

"Person X said Y on date Z."

Do not treat social-media claims as independently verified facts.

## TIER 5 — OPEN WEB

Use only for discovery and supporting evidence.

Never let an anonymous blog become the sole evidence for a major political fact.

# 5. AFFIDAVITS

Use at least TWO sources.

PRIMARY:

ECI candidate affidavit system.

SECONDARY:

ADR / MyNeta

https://www.myneta.info/

MyNeta contains candidate affidavit information including financial, criminal and educational disclosures and has historical election coverage.

Capture:

* assets
* liabilities
* income
* profession
* education
* criminal cases
* spouse assets
* dependent assets
* property
* vehicles
* jewellery
* investments
* bank deposits where legally/publicly disclosed
* loans
* government dues
* tax liabilities
* contracts
* business interests

IMPORTANT:

Never call affidavit assets "actual net worth."

Use:

**Declared Assets**

and separately:

**Declared Liabilities**

Then calculate:

Declared Assets − Declared Liabilities

and label it:

**Approximate Declared Net Position**

Do not represent it as actual wealth.

# 100. THE GOLDEN RULE

Political Eye should never answer:

"Because the internet says so."

It should answer:

"Here is what the official record says.

Here is what independent research says.

Here is what reputable reporting says.

Here is where the sources agree.

Here is where they disagree.

Here is the evidence.

Here is what Political Eye can and cannot establish."

That distinction is what will separate Political Eye from every generic political-information website.

The goal is not to collect the most webpages.

The goal is to build the **most defensible political evidence graph for India.**
